# Rubble square — phase 1 review

Ground, one dusk panorama and painted skyline are integrated on
`art/rubble-square`. Main was pulled at cc0409b and merged into this worktree
before continuing. The owner's dirty env-corner-a.blend on main was not touched.
This is a phase-1 delivery, not completion of the full six-phase art pass.
Production asset rows remain at **review**, with the acceptance gaps below.

## Executed and observed

- Generated a top-down cobble texture, game-height hero and material-ID; retained
  all four rejected ground sheets. No generated sheet was rescaled or laid out
  programmatically. Ground source and atlas were profiled before integration.
- Generated backdrop sheets until the shared-scale layout passed, then hero
  and ID. An eye-height render exposed floating lower edges in the first model;
  the design was retired and the sheet/hero/ID regenerated for ground-reaching
  flats. All six rejected backdrop sheets and both rejected atlas generations
  remain in the reference packet, with STATUS and exact prompts.
- Authored/exported the ground and backdrop through an isolated Blender MCP
  server on port 9877; original Blender on 9876 was left alone. Sources contain
  the five named pipeline collections, calibration capsules and review cameras.
  `scripts/blender/run-rubble-mcp.py` is the transport; the adjacent asset scripts
  record the actual construction. No transform_apply was used.
- Ground: 28 total triangles (16 visible, 12 unchanged collision), one 1024
  MAT_Plaster albedo. Two tram-scar ribbons, no raised walking obstacles.
  Backdrop: 163 triangles, 16 flat meshes in radius-26/40 m octagons, one
  MAT_TimberSoot 1024 albedo; 1 spire, 1 four-legged tower, 6 chimneys, 8 gables.
  Source of these counts and dimensions: `test/glb.test.js`, `glb-final.txt`,
  `scripts/blender/rubble-backdrop.py` and `backdrop-build.json`.
- `scripts/capture-reviews.mjs` produced all six fixed captures for each GLB in
  `design/reviews/env-ground-a/` and `design/reviews/env-backdrop-a/`. The fixed
  cameras, scale rulers, silhouette and collider overlays were inspected.
  Supplemental actual-square stills are `spire-square.png`, `tower-square.png`
  and `sky-square.png`, from `scripts/review-rubble-phase-1.mjs` at eye height
  1.7 m. These expose a temporary review camera only through a browser route;
  no review hook is added to production code. The early failure is retained in
  `backdrop-raised-failure.png`.
- Runtime preserves both backdrop texture maps and their authored blue colour
  multiplier. The sky is a cached 4096 × 1024 sRGB panorama through assets.js;
  its luminance modulates the existing state gradient. `browser-review.json`
  records actual decoded dimensions, all 16 backdrop maps, successful missing
  node refusal, panorama-abort gradient fallback, linear tone and reduced
  motion. No light intensities, controller measurements or engine code changed.
- Final `npm run verify` output: `verify.txt`. Content grep output:
  `content.txt`. The grep covers paths, source/comments and GLB metadata; it
  does not inspect image pixels. Generated images were also visually inspected
  for content marks; no prohibited marks were observed in the new art.

## Measurements and exact methods

`comparison.json` and `comparison.txt` are generated by
`scripts/compare-rubble-phase-1.mjs`. It asserts raw byte equality of both
replay files and all placed collider/socket records. Input snapshots come from
`scripts/measure-rubble-contract.mjs`; this source geometry audit skips image
decoding, which the browser review tests separately. The complete deterministic
replay hash is `9313643a2934c2c74eeb64eb79eeeaaf75ab2e31744502b0bb6307e1f2c459a7`.
All 32 placement records, controller tuning and square constants equal baseline.
This is complementary evidence, not a substitute for a walk.html measurement.

Source: `scripts/profile-rubble-texture.py`, `cobble-atlas-profile.json` and
`backdrop-atlas-profile.json`. Source generations were also measured. The
spectral gate measures horizontal luma period across 16 bands, 3–24 cycles per
image, uses a Theil–Sen slope and 4096 deterministic permutations, and rejects
p < .01 or absolute robust drift ≥ .10. This is a practical detector of
systematic perspective drift, not proof that the mathematical slope is exactly
zero. `scripts/test-rubble-profile.py` and `profile-controls.json` record a
constant-period control passing and a converging-period control failing.

Sky: `scripts/profile-rubble-sky.py`, `sky-runtime-profile.json`: maximum
within-10° warmth change 0.024414, adjacent-window mean change 0.017749, both
below 0.05; per-column luma max/min 1.036878, below 2. The production panorama
is 4096 × 1024; source was 1774 × 887 and was resampled in Blender, not generated
at native production resolution. No seam repair. This panorama is the brief's
special-size resource, not an additional 1024 material-role atlas.

Sheet: `scripts/measure-rubble-sheet.py` reports front/side 401 × 130 px against
400 × 130 expected at 5 px/m; top 399 × 399 against 400 × 400, within the same
2 px tolerance. It measures native generated pixels, not a corrected copy.
The two octagons establish panel count; overlapping elevations do not prove
every hidden motif. The GLB checks the complete authored part schedule.

## Visual comparison and judgment

Held against the four frames in `design/concepts/rubble`, rather than against
style adjectives: the dusk-square frame supplies slate cobbles and damaged
church hierarchy; the day queue frame supplies matte mineral wear; the trial
frame supplies a quiet roof silhouette behind scarce warm attention; the
citizen lineup supplies the limit on environmental detail competing with
people. Facades, citizens and furniture in the current runtime remain their
pre-pass forms and are not represented as matching those references yet.

| Asset | Five visible correspondences to its hero | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ground | staggered rectangular courses; rounded painted stone edges; dark grout; sparse muted vegetation/grime; two parallel tram scars | Scar repetition is visible near camera; weeds are small painted marks, not individually identifiable plants. The fixed distant slab view cannot judge those fine details alone. |
| Backdrop | narrow church spire; squared tower tank; four leg gaps; capped chimney cuts; broken gable silhouette | The exact spire damage differs between hero and model; the low fixed silhouette view occludes the far spire. Supplemental actual-square views show it. Two rings overlap; not every motif is visible in one frame. |
| Sky | broad quiet cloud paint; horizontal brush tendency; low contrast; large empty areas; soft horizon-to-upper-sky transition | Source hue is deliberately not reproduced literally; existing state gradients still control runtime colour. Fine source grain is not readable at every game angle. |

These are author observations, not independent blind acceptance. The sky is
subtle and the repeated ground brush tile is visible on close inspection.
The corrected scenery reaches the ground, so looking up between roofs no
longer exposes the original floating bands. The broad painted flats remain
visibly flat, as required by the theatre direction.

## Open acceptance gaps

1. **Night ground floor fails.** `scripts/measure-rubble-ground-floor.mjs`
   measured the untouched branch-point ground at `(0,1,4)` (257,223 visible
   ground pixels); `scripts/review-rubble-phase-1.mjs` measured phase 1 at
   `(0,1,2)` (264,931 pixels), versus the historical `(1,11,20)` requirement.
   These are minimum-luma pixels, not component minima. Phase-1 component
   minima are `(0,1,1)`. The method renders a ground ID mask with occluders,
   erodes one pixel, then samples the unmodified screenshot. This shows an
   existing gap plus a darker phase-1 minimum; it is not waived because the
   warm ceiling passes. Phase 5 must resolve it against the frozen baseline.
2. The complete walkable contract table has not been re-measured in walk.html.
   All source collider/socket records and tuning are equal, but that does not
   certify passage/ledge behaviour through the browser workbench.
3. Phase 2 facades/rubble/pump, phase 3 furniture/posters/lamps/searchlight,
   phase 4 eight static citizens, and final phase 5 lighting remain outstanding.
   Citizens must preserve no rig/clips, foot-centre root and SOCKET_label,
   with the corrected 1.6k triangle limit per variant.
4. Phase 6 blind 8/8 citizen silhouette identification, independent pasted-on
   review at three fog distances, full-pass hero/model comparisons, final
   before/after/verify/content evidence under rubble-v1 remain outstanding.
   This packet is phase-1 author review, not a claim those tests passed.
5. GPT Image 2 was requested in every generation prompt; the built-in tool did
   not return the actual model identifier, so exact model identity is unverified.
6. The generated source textures are uniformly reduced to 1024 in Blender;
   the sky is resampled to its stated aspect ratio. Generated sheets remain
   native and unmodified. The statistical spectral gate and overlapping sheet
   counts have the limits stated above. No independent pixel-content review
   was performed. Mobile remains unclaimed.

Inferences: the chosen blue multipliers and quiet surface detail support the
mood-frame hierarchy; source equality supports an expectation of unchanged
movement. Neither inference replaces the outstanding visual and walk tests.
